# Emission vs Revocation (rule 1.b)

# Context

- paper review (2606-vcs, item 2.7): paper says +1K rewards posts
  that "other members tolerate"
- impl (`src/freechains/chain/rules.lua`, consolidation scan):
    - +1K granted 24h after post time, once a day per member
    - no check on dislikes, post reps, or revoked state
- decision: tie emission to revocation only, not to dislikes
- paper side tracked in `2606-vcs/.claude/plans/260907-revoke.md`

# Rule

- a consolidated post holds +1K for its author while not revoked
- revoked = `is_revoked(act)`: member or community net revoke < 0
- dislikes alone never affect emission (they already hit the author)

# Cases

- revoke before 24h (post still "12-24")
    - at 24h: post consolidates, but no credit (`earned=false`)
    - later unrevoke: grant +1K then, subject to once-a-day
- revoke after 24h (post already credited, `earned=true`)
    - on revoke: author loses 1K (`earned=false`)
    - on unrevoke: author regains 1K (`earned=true`, no daily check)
- flip-flop bounded by revoke/unrevoke cost (min 1000 each, taxed)
- refund of 500 (rule 2) unchanged: rate limiting, not tolerance
- unsigned begs: consolidate, no credit (as today)
