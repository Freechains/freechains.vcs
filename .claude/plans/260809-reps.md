# Reps: parameter values, thinking of users

## Summary

Are the economy's constants right for real users?
Review of starting reps, daily earn, cost, tax, cap —
what works, what bites — plus the pending NO DEBT gate
work (moved here from `280808-redesign.md`).

## Constants today

| constant     | value             | note                  |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------------------|
| unit         | 1000 internal     | CLI speaks internal   |
| start supply | 30000 per chain   | split among pioneers  |
| cap          | 30000 per author  | rule 4.b              |
| post cost    | 1000 per file op  | refunded after 0-12h  |
| daily earn   | +1000 per day     | consolidation only    |
| tax          | 10% burned        | every vote            |
| post-like    | 50/50 author/post | after tax             |

## Good — the ratios to keep

- **cap = pioneer grant (30000)**: a newcomer reaches
  pioneer power in ~30 days; no aristocracy; at cap the
  +1000/day is clipped, so hoarding is pointless — reps
  are only useful spent
- **dislikes cost more than they destroy**: caster pays
  `n`, target loses `0.9n` (author) or `0.45n` (post);
  griefing is a net loss; a 30000 nuke bricks the
  attacker for ~as long as the victim (+1000/day each);
  harassment self-defeats with no moderator
- **refundable post cost + variable discount**: active
  chains post for free (ratio >= 0.5 -> 0h); quiet
  chains get the 12h float throttle; per-file-op cost
  (rule 5) makes mass edits/deletes self-limiting
- **mint only via time**: votes transfer, never create;
  supply ~ `30000 + authors*days*1000 - burns`; no
  like-farming inflation to find; the 10% tax is then
  belt-and-suspenders (everyone capped anyway), its real
  job is making vote-wars lossy
- **vote affordability gate**
  (`done/260722-bug-reps-debt.md`): no debt, no
  self-brick

## Bad — where the numbers bite

1. **newcomer arithmetic** (dust holes -> NO DEBT below):
   "one like = one post" fails; after tax + split the
   cheapest grant that posts NOW is author-like 1112 or
   post-like 2223 (vs 1000 direct cost); a beg-like of
   1000 delivers 450 and only posts after the discounted
   12h credit; nobody discovers any of this from the CLI
   -> NO DEBT closes the dust admission; the sponsorship
   price table must be documented (README/guide)
2. **pioneer split max/N breaks at large N** — RESOLVED:
   reject the genesis. The bound is the post gate itself:
   at most `max // cost` = 100 pioneers, else
   "chains add : too many pioneers" -- no extra constant,
   since a founder who cannot post in their own chain is
   the only broken case the mechanics name.
   Chosen over a per-pioneer floor to keep the invariant
   "initial supply = one cap"; a 101st founder is admitted
   by a like seconds later
3. **discount ratio counts the author's own follow-up** —
   WON'T DO: the refund is the author's own cost returning,
   and the ratio is proportional to reps held, so a majority
   holder unthrottling themselves is the same power they
   already hold over consensus and votes (sybils do not
   help: splitting is taxed and shrinks each share)
   - side effect kept: a `reps` query has no signer, so it
     is more pessimistic than the very next action by the
     same author (query 49500 vs act-then-spend 40000 in
     the README); cosmetic, documented if it confuses

## Fine — leave alone

- **+1000/day**: 30-day ramp to full member is a
  reasonable trust period; sponsorship capacity ~1
  newcomer per member per 2-3 days — organic growth by
  design, not viral
- **10% tax**: low enough not to hurt honest transfers;
  higher buys nothing (mint is time-based)
- **12h / 24h windows**: the original freechains rule;
  one grant slot per day keeps supply predictable
- **128 KB size cap**: orthogonal, fine

## Resolved already

- ext truncation lied to users ("reps 0 but can post"):
  REPS UNITS (280808) killed the ext seam — exact
  integers end to end

## DONE: new parameter values

| constant     | today  | new    | note                    |
|--------------|--------|--------|-------------------------|
| cap          | 30000  | 50000  | 100 posts of runway     |
| post cost    | 1000   | 500    | refunded at 12h         |
| standard like| 1000   | 1000   | unchanged (CLI usage)   |
| daily mint   | = cost | 1000   | NEW `earn`, decoupled   |

- decouple mint from cost: the 12h credit is a REFUND (stays
  `cost`), the 24h credit is the MINT (own `earn` constant)
    - else halving cost halves income: 100-day ramp to cap
    - `earn` 1000 -> 50-day ramp; "earn a like a day, a post
      costs half a like"
- beg-like keeps the 50/50 split (REVERTED: no special case)
    - a 1000 beg-like ADMITS the post (enters main, 450 score)
      but HOLDS the author at 450 < 500: probation, they speak
      when the 12h refund lands (sooner if the chain is active)
    - immediate speech costs the sponsor 1112 (-> exactly 500)
    - keeps the credit path uniform and a vote's value
      conserved (author + post = the post-tax amount)
    - side effect: at 450 the author may beg again while
      waiting
- NO DEBT gate becomes `reps >= 500` (see below)
- pioneer limit follows from the split: N > 100 leaves each
  below the post gate -> genesis rejected (see bad 2)
- DESIGN STATEMENT (deliberate, not a side effect): a like now
  costs 2 posts (was 1) -- and the post cost returns at 12h
  while the like is spent for good. Speak freely, endorse
  deliberately; dampens vote-spam
- unchanged: tax 10%, `÷ split` for non-beg likes, 12/24h
  windows, griefing ratios (full-cap nuke: attacker 50 days to
  recover, victim 45)

## NO DEBT: cost-quantized gates

(moved from `280808-redesign.md`; red tests first; reps
units landed first, so the dust holes are OPEN in the
CLI now — NO DEBT must ship before sync/release)

- invariant: entering maturation costs >= `cost`, paid
  by the author (post) or the sponsor (beg-like)
- post gate `reps > 0` -> `reps >= cost`
    - today 1 unit = a full 1000-cost posting right (to
      -999): author-like of 2 -> sybil gets 1 -> posts;
      30000 cap = ~15000 sybil posts vs 30 direct (500x)
    - `>= cost` restores lossy transfers (cheapest useful
      grant: 1112 author-like / 2223 post-like vs 1000
      direct); negative balances from posting die
    - matches the vote rule ("afford full magnitude")
- beg gate `reps <= 0` -> `reps < cost` (else 1..999
  holders can neither post nor beg)
- beg-like floor: positive like on a beg requires
  `n >= cost` — admission mints future income (+cost per
  post at 12h, +cost/24h author ratchet) and its price
  must not be dust; the sponsor pays the entry fee the
  begger couldn't
- discounts untouched: they only shift WHEN the 12h
  credit lands; credits are cost-quantized, so the first
  one alone crosses the post gate (beg-like 1000 ->
  begger 450 now, posts after discounted 12h; immediate:
  beg-like >= 2223 or author-like >= 1112 top-up)
- old ext CLI masked both holes: min like = 1000
