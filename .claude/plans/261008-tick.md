# Goal

- one chain clock (tick) replaces the per-post 12h/24h timers
- tick period adapts to activity: busy chains (chat, 2-day
  conference) grow fast; quiet ones keep `full` (24h)
- rule 1.b stays "at most one reward per member per period":
  volume mints nothing
- no per-chain constant, no per-post or per-member pendings

# Evidence (2606-sims, chat 5k, 26/10/08)

- 5000 posts in 35 days, 58 members, top member 1081 posts
- one settle per member per 24h: 4295 of 5000 posts still
  queued at the end (offline estimate: 87%)
    - same estimate with 1h: 25%; with 10 min: 0.5%
- reps of a heavy member climb 1K/day after the corpus ends
  (31.5K at end, 50K cap at +30 days)
- `reps` at a far `--now` folds every queued settle:
    - +1 day 0.15 s, +30 days 1.7 s, +16 years 22 s
- 2-day conference, 500 users: today admits ~250 at most
  (50K pioneers / 556 per like, +90K on day 2)

# Today (rules.lua `advance`)

- per post: `maturity` 00-12 / 12-24, `time.member`
- refund 500 after `C.time.half * max(0, 1 - 2*ratio)`
    - ratio = reps of members acting after the post / total
- settle: per member, oldest 12-24 record once
  `time >= head + C.time.full`, one slot per `C.time.full`
    - grid `last + full`: catches up after idle gaps
    - queue: every record waits its turn, paid 1K/day
      forever (per-post emission, delayed; reward
      without presence)
- `ordered` sort + discount walk over all pending posts
  at every `advance`

# Rule (tick)

- chain clock since genesis: a tick ends
  `C.time.full * max(0, 1 - 2*r)` after the previous one
    - `r` = reps of members acting since the tick starts /
      reps of all members (same ratio as today, anchored
      at the tick)
    - the half must come from at least two members
- at a tick, each member with a non-revoked post since the
  previous tick earns +1K
    - the first such post (order) carries the credit; its
      revoke state moves the +1K (as today)
    - second post in the same tick: nothing
- refund: same clock at `C.time.half`: all post costs
  since the previous half tick refund at once
    - or one clock at `half`: refund at the next tick,
      reward at every second tick (one sequence, both
      constants)
- membership of a post in a tick by `time.apply`
  (consensus position), not by declared time
- `--now` far ahead: ticks with r = 0 every `full` after
  the last action; nothing pending after the first

# Why (discussed 26/10/08)

- adaptive slot per member hits 0 at r >= 50%: 10 posts,
  one acknowledgement, 10 rewards
- per-member anchor at the last reward fixes it, but keeps
  per-post windows, a parked post, member anchors
- global tick: same emission bound (1K per tick per
  member; a tick needs half of the reps to act), with
  one state per chain
- per-post judgment window was never real in busy chains;
  the clawback (rule 1.b) covers both cases
- late losing branch: position timestamps flatten it into
  one or two ticks, farmed identities earn once
- 24h vs any constant: arbitrary; what matters is per
  period vs per post

# Behavior

- quiet chain: r ~ 0, ticks every 24h, daily payroll
- busy chat: ticks of minutes to hours, 1K per tick
- conference: short ticks, exponential admission
- 31 posts in one tick: one paid, 30 earn nothing
- member idle then two posts in a tick: one reward
- post just before a tick: paid and refunded at once
  (phase is public; revoke still claws back)

# Economics (checked 26/10/08)

- implicit acknowledgement: only later activity of reputed
  members shortens the tick; a lone spammer waits 24h
- majority holder: cannot tick alone (two-member rule);
  two pioneers must both act for a tick
- revoke claws back the +1K even after the tick (rule 1.b),
  plus 45% of the revoke weight from the author
    - revoked spam nets the spammer -450 per post
- revoke floor stays 1000 (`260831-min-vote.md`: burying
  costs more than speech)

# Implementation

- `G.tick`: `{ full = start, half = start }` (or one)
- per advance: members acting since the tick start and
  their reps, incremental; one ratio per clock
- at a tick: walk posts since the tick start once
  (bounded by one tick), credit/refund, mark credited
- drop: `maturity`, `time.member`, `members[*].time`,
  `ordered`, the per-post discount walk
- consensus replay: ticks are a pure function of the
  order, recomputed after a reorg

# Open

- two-member rule vs excluding the largest actor vs floor
- one clock (`half`, reward every second tick) or two
- `time.apply` vs declared time inside a tick for the
  "first post" of a member
- unsigned begs: refund/credit nothing (as today)
- docs: reps.md (ln 45-49, 108, 138), paper

# Paper (main.tex)

- 398 rule 1.b: "after 24h, at most once a day" -> "at the
  next tick, at most once per tick"; 404 rule 2 likewise
- 638, 655 T2/T3: refund and reward at the chain ticks,
  24h/12h at most, sooner with reputed activity
- 662: the tick is the block interval; activity sets the
  difficulty; emission per tick per author, not per post
- 932 chain time: "up to 12h / 24h"
- 1059 farming: "once per tick, 24h in isolation";
  merged late branches flatten into one tick
- one phrase: members may pace posts to the tick
- keep "consolidate"; "settle" is the 7-day window

# Won't do

- per-chain time scale (`time.full` per chain)
- revoke at 500: weakens the censorship price
- per-member slot scaled by `1 - 2r`: period 0 in busy chains
- per-member anchor at the last reward + parked post:
  more state for the same bound
  (branch `261008-todo-drop-adaptive-settle`)
- unbounded queue: per-post emission, delayed
- floor at 12h: keeps the backlog, blocks the conference
